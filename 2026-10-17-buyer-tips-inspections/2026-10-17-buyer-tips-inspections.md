## Slide copy
1. [Band: UNDER CONTRACT? INSPECT FIRST.] BOOK YOUR INSPECTION RIGHT AWAY. "Once you're under contract, the clock starts. Here's how I recommend using your inspection window." (B)
2. THE MARKET, SouthPark, as of October 2026: median list $625,000; 581 active (+13.16% YoY); 56 median days on market; 98% sale-to-list. "More homes to compare than a year ago. Know the condition of the one you choose." Source: Realtor.com Economic Research, SouthPark page, Oct 2026 (A)
3. NC: USE YOUR DUE DILIGENCE PERIOD: on NC's standard contract (Form 2-T), you can end the deal for any reason during this period (C); that's the window for inspections, specialists and repair talks (my tip); the due diligence fee is generally non-refundable, so have inspectors lined up (C)
4. SC: KNOW YOUR CONTRACT'S DATES: your purchase contract sets the inspection terms and deadlines (B); if it's contingent on a satisfactory inspection, you may be able to cancel without penalty (B); put every deadline on your calendar the day you sign (my tip)
5. HIRE A LICENSED INSPECTOR: NC paid home inspections require a license from the NC Home Inspector Licensure Board, G.S. 143-151.50 (D); SC home inspectors must be licensed by the SC Residential Builders Commission, S.C. Code 40-59-520 (F); choose someone accountable to you (B)
6. BE THERE AND ASK QUESTIONS: attending makes the report easier to understand (B); ask what wasn't inspected, and in SC the report must note it, S.C. Code 40-59-560 (F); take notes and photos as you walk (my tip)
7. READ THE WHOLE REPORT: NC report is due by the agreed date, or within 3 business days, G.S. 143-151.58 (E); NC's summary page says it plainly: it is not the entire report (E); items flagged for a specialist deserve a closer look (E, my wording)
8. ASK ABOUT RADON: EPA recommends knowing the radon level in any home you consider buying; ask the seller for past results, and if there's no test yet, have it tested; EPA: fix the home if radon is 4 pCi/L or higher (G)
9. THEN NEGOTIATE CALMLY: you can ask the seller to make repairs or give a credit; the seller may or may not agree, depending on your contract and the market; your lender may require some repairs before closing (B)
10. MY RECOMMENDATION: PICK YOUR INSPECTOR BEFORE YOU OFFER. "I recommend it so the first days of your window go to answers, not phone calls. Let's plan it together." + contact block: Denise Wilson | REALTOR® / denisewilson.lircarolinas.com / 704-615-7347 / (blank) / Equal Housing Opportunity
