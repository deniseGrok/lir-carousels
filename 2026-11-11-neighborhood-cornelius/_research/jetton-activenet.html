Jetton Park
DescriptionJetton Park is a 104 acre park located in the town of Cornelius on the shores of Lake Norman. The park features a playground, large decks, sandy beach area, tennis courts, walking trails, and biking trails. This park also offers an indoor shelter that is reservable. The amenities of this parkare reservable for group use. There is an entry fee of $3 (county) and $5 (non-county) on weekends and holidays at this location.
