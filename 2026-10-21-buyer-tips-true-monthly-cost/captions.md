## LinkedIn
When buyers compare homes, I recommend comparing the full monthly cost, not just the list price. Here's why. A mortgage payment often includes more than principal and interest. Many lenders collect property taxes and homeowner's insurance through an escrow account, and the CFPB notes that both can change from year to year, so the payment can change too. A conventional loan with less than 20% down may require private mortgage insurance; borrowers can ask in writing to cancel it once the balance is scheduled to reach 80% of the home's original value, and it generally ends automatically at 78% if payments are current. Every lender must send a Loan Estimate within three business days of an application, on the same standard form, which makes side-by-side comparison easier. And in South Carolina, a primary home the owner lives in can be assessed at 4% instead of 6%, but the owner has to apply through the county assessor. Inventory is up from a year ago: Realtor.com shows active listings up 16.8% in Mecklenburg County and 9.7% in York County, SC, in September 2026. That gives buyers room to compare carefully.
