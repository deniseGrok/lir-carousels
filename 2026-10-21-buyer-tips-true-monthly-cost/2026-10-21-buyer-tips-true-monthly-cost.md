## Slides
1. Hook band "BUYING? KNOW YOUR REAL PAYMENT." + "The price isn't your payment" (payment = loan + taxes + insurance + any mortgage insurance/HOA dues)
2. Market (stats only, navy, no photo): Mecklenburg NC median list $444,750 (-6.4% YoY), 4,389 active (+16.8% YoY); York SC median list $419,972 (-0.8% YoY), 1,201 active (+9.7% YoY); Sep 2026. Source line (only slide with one).
3. Read your Loan Estimate: within 3 business days of application; shows rate, payment, closing costs, est. taxes and insurance; same form for every lender
4. Taxes and insurance can change: escrow; taxes/premiums can change yearly; get current tax bill + insurance quote before offering
5. Plan for mortgage insurance: conventional <20% down may require PMI; request cancellation at 80% of original value (in writing); generally automatic at 78% if current; FHA/VA differ
6. Ask about HOA dues: amount/frequency/coverage, planned special assessments, how they're paid (advice only)
7. Buying in SC? Apply for the 4%: primary home assessed at 4% vs 6%; not automatic, apply via county assessor; proof such as SC driver's license, vehicle registration, tax return
8. Budget for utilities and upkeep (advice only)
9. Leave room in your budget: preapproval = what a lender may lend, not what feels comfortable; keep reserves (advice only)
10. My recommendation: "Shop by the monthly number" + contact block (Denise Wilson | REALTOR® / denisewilson.lircarolinas.com / 704-615-7347 / blank / Equal Housing Opportunity)
