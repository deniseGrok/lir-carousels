## Instagram
🏡 Thinking about a home in an HOA or condo community?

I always tell buyers: the disclosure form is the start — the governing documents are where the real rules and costs live.

💰 Do you know what the dues actually cover?
📊 Have you asked what's in reserve?
📄 Do the rental, pet, and parking rules fit your plans?
🛡️ Do you know the association insurance and any transfer fees?
📋 Any open violations, judgments, or pending lawsuits?

Surprises after closing are expensive. Diligence is your window to find them.

A pretty amenity list is not the same as a healthy budget.

## Facebook personal profile
🏡 Friends, if you're house hunting in an HOA or condo community, this one's for you!

I always tell my buyers: the disclosure form is the start — the governing documents are where the real rules and costs live. Here's what I walk them through.

💰 Match the dues to what they actually cover
📊 Ask what's in reserve and whether a special assessment is approved
📄 Read rental, pet, parking, and use rules before you offer
🛡️ Confirm association insurance, deductibles, and every transfer fee
📋 Request recent minutes and ask about violations or lawsuits

Surprises after closing are expensive. Diligence is your window.

A pretty amenity list is not the same as a healthy budget.
