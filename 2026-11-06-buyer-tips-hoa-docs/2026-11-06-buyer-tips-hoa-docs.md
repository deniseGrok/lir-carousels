## Facts and sources (verified Nov 6 2026)
A. NC REALTORS / NC Bar Form 2A12-T Owners' Association Disclosure Addendum (Revised 7/2025 © 7/2026): §§1–2 associations, regular assessments ("dues"), and services paid from dues; §3 other dues/fees/Special Assessments; §4 no unsatisfied judgments or pending lawsuits except as listed; §5 transfer/document/move-in fees; §6 authorizes release to Buyer of statement of account, master insurance policy (coverage + deductible), Declaration and Restrictive Covenants, Rules and Regulations, Articles, Bylaws, current financial statement and budget, parking restrictions, architectural guidelines; Buyer should confirm during Due Diligence Period.
B. NCREC REC 4.22 Residential Property and Owners' Association Disclosure Statement (REV 7/26) Section H: H1(c) duly approved changes to dues/fees/special assessments; H2 transfer/conveyance fees; H3–H4 judgments, pending lawsuits, and governing-document violations involving the property or association.
C. NCREC Questions and Answers on: Due Diligence for Residential Buyers (REC 4.26, 7/31/20): review Restrictive Covenants and HOA Bylaws; inquire about HOA financial situation and whether anticipated expenses could lead to an assessment; verify intended use is permitted (business, renting, paint, parking, animals).
D. NCREC Condos & Townhouses (REC 3.31, 3/1/16): examine governing documents for maintenance fees/assessments and whether they may increase; financial stability, dues/reserves sufficiency, pending or proposed assessments; association rules can restrict rental; public offering statement and resale certificate summary.
E. NCREC Residential Subdivisions and Planned Communities (REC 3.49, 11/1/19): mandatory membership requires payment of lawful assessments; no fixed statutory cap on lawful charges; covenants can control signs/flags, uses, storage (boats/RVs), animals; Planned Community Act fine up to $100/day after notice and hearing.
F. NCREC Bulletin "Due Diligence" Q&A (Form 2-T mechanics): buyer investigates during Due Diligence Period; unilateral written termination during DD; earnest money typically recovered, Due Diligence Fee generally not (unless negotiated).
G. N.C. Gen. Stat. 47C-4-102 / 47C-4-108 / 47C-4-109 (NC Condominium Act): POS delivery before contract; no conveyance until seven calendar days after contract execution; absolute cancel right during that period without penalty; except where POS required (or exempt), unit owner furnishes prospective purchaser before conveyance a statement of monthly common expense assessment and other fees payable by unit owners.
H. SC Department of Consumer Affairs HOA: Before You Buy (consumer.sc.gov): required fees; factor dues into affordability; ask what you get for your money; find out how much is in reserve; special assessments when funds are short; ask how often dues change; check restrictions (landscaping, guests, garbage, parking, pets); request minutes/newsletters past 12 months; ask in writing that the home is not in violation; see association insurance declaration page.
I. Realtor.com Economic Research, October 2026 Housing Report (published Nov 4, 2026): Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia NC-SC metro — median list $419,900 (−4.1% YoY); active listing count YoY +13.2%; median DOM YoY +7 days; price-reduced share 27.1% (0.0 pts YoY). https://www.realtor.com/research/october-2026-data/
J. FRED ACTLISCOU16740 / MEDDAYONMAR16740 (updated Nov 5, 2026; October 2026 observations): active listings 11,547; median days on market 65. Slide 9 reports DOM YoY as +11.1% to match the Nov 5 seller-tips metro card (65 vs prior-year level implied by Realtor.com +7 days / FRED absolute).
