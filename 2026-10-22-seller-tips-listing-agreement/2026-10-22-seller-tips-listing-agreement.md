## Facts and sources (all fetched and read Oct 22 2026, ~11:14-11:25 AM ET)
A. 21 NCAC 58A .0104 (NCREC): (a) every agreement for brokerage services with an owner must be in writing and signed at formation; for a definite period; terminates without prior notice at expiration; early-termination penalty set out clearly and conspicuously; (b) listing agreements contain the nondiscrimination provision; (c) "Working with Real Estate Agents" at first substantial contact, reviewed with the consumer; (d) a broker representing one party may not represent another party in the transaction without the written authority of each party. http://reports.oah.state.nc.us/ncac/title%2021%20-%20occupational%20licensing%20boards%20and%20commissions/chapter%2058%20-%20real%20estate%20commission/subchapter%20a/21%20ncac%2058a%20.0104.pdf
   Slide 8 wording "one firm may represent both buyer and seller only with each party's written authority" = .0104(d) plus (i) (a firm representing more than one party is a dual agent).
B. S.C. Code 40-57-135(I)(2): listing agreement must be in writing with all material terms incl. (a) duties/services and office policy on dual agency, designated agency and transaction brokerage; (b) compensation amount or method; (c) how and when earned; (d) how divided among cooperating brokers; (f) specific beginning and ending dates; (h) terminates on the definite expiration date unless a written extension is signed; (k) copy to the seller at or directly following signing. https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t40c057.php
C. S.C. Code 40-57-370(A): Disclosure of Brokerage Relationships form at the first practical opportunity to potential buyers and sellers with substantive contact. Same URL.
D. NAR, "Compensation, Commission and Concessions" (Aug 13 2024): commissions not set by law, negotiable, memorialized in a written agreement; offers of compensation cannot be communicated on MLSs but, with the seller's approval, can be shared through flyers, brokerage websites and other marketing; seller concessions can still be communicated on an MLS but cannot be conditioned on payment to a buyer broker; practice changes effective Aug 17 2024. https://www.nar.realtor/news/real-estate-news/sales-marketing/compensation-commission-and-concessions
