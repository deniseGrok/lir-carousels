## LinkedIn
A listing agreement is the seller's contract with the brokerage firm that markets the home, and I recommend reading every page before signing. In North Carolina, NCREC Rule 58A .0104 requires it to be in writing and signed when it's formed, to run for a definite period and to end at expiration without notice, and any early-termination penalty must be clear and conspicuous. In South Carolina, S.C. Code 40-57-135 requires the agreement to spell out the firm's duties, the fee or how it's calculated, when it's earned, how it's shared with cooperating brokers, and specific start and end dates, and it ends on the expiration date unless the seller signs a written extension. Commissions aren't set by law; they're negotiated and put in writing. Since Aug. 17, 2024, offers of compensation to buyer agents can't be shown on an MLS, though with the seller's approval they can be shared in other marketing.
