## Instagram
🏡 You found the house… have you priced the insurance yet?

Your lender will generally ask for proof of homeowners insurance before closing, so it helps to shop early.

🛡️ What does the policy cover, and what does it leave out?
📄 Has the home had past insurance claims?
🔨 Does it pay replacement cost or actual cash value?
💰 What are the deductibles, including wind or hail?
🏘️ If there's an HOA, what does its insurance policy cover?

The answers can shape your monthly payment and how protected you are after you move in.

The right home matters. The right coverage protects it.
