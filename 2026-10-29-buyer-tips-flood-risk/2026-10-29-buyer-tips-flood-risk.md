## Facts and sources (verified Oct 29 2026)
A. FEMA Flood Map Service Center, search by address: https://msc.fema.gov/portal/home (slide 3)
B. NC Flood Risk Information System (FRIS): https://fris.nc.gov/fris/ (slide 3)
C. FEMA flood zone definitions: high-risk zones begin with A or V; shaded X moderate, unshaded X minimal; floods can happen anywhere. FEMA glossary "Flood Zones" https://www.fema.gov/about/glossary/flood-zones (slide 4)
D. Lender requirement: federally regulated / government-backed mortgages in a high-risk (special flood hazard) area require flood insurance; lenders may require it elsewhere. FEMA "Myths and Facts About Flood Insurance" https://www.fema.gov/fact-sheet/myths-and-facts-about-flood-insurance ; FloodSmart (slide 5)
E. Homeowners policies generally don't cover flood; flood coverage via NFIP or private insurers. FEMA Myths and Facts (above); FloodSmart.gov (slide 6)
F. 30-day waiting period; no wait when the initial purchase is in connection with making a mortgage loan. NFIP Flood Insurance Manual (Oct 2025) Sec. III https://nfipservices.floodsmart.gov/sites/default/files/media/document/2025-09/fema_nfip-FloodInsuranceManual-October2025-508c.pdf ; FEMA FAQ https://www.fema.gov/faq/waiting-period-activating-flood-policy (slide 7)
G. NC Residential Property and Owners' Association Disclosure Statement (REC 4.22) flood questions (flood zone, flood damage/claims, flood insurance, elevation certificate, "No" = no actual knowledge; existing flood policy may be assignable). NCREC https://www.ncrec.gov/forms/repdis.pdf ; FloodSmart Q&A for Real Estate Professionals (Aug 2025: seller can transfer policy) https://www.agents.floodsmart.gov/sites/default/files/media/document/2025-09/questions-answers-real-estate-professionals-brochure-08-2025.pdf (slides 8, 9)
