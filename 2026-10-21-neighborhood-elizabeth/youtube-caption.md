## Title
Elizabeth: Charlotte's Second Streetcar Suburb #Shorts

## Description
Oak-lined streets, 1920s bungalows and Charlotte's first public park, about a mile east of Uptown.

· Elizabeth takes its name from Elizabeth College, which began building here in 1897.
· It grew from five subdivisions laid out between 1891 and 1915 and joined the National Register of Historic Places in 1989.
· Independence Park, designed by John Nolen, reopened in 2023 after a nearly $6 million county renovation.
· The CityLYNX Gold Line streetcar stops at Elizabeth & Hawthorne, Hawthorne & 5th St., 8th St. and Sunnyside Ave.
