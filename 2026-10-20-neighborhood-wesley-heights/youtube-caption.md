## Title
Wesley Heights: Charlotte's 1920s Streetcar Suburb #Shorts

## Description
Bungalows, a streetcar stop and a greenway, about a mile from Uptown Charlotte.

· Wesley Heights was first laid out in 1911 on the Wadsworth family's model farm, after the West Trade Street trolley line was extended nearby.
· It has been on the National Register of Historic Places since 1995, with Bungalow, Craftsman, Tudor Revival and Colonial Revival homes.
· The Wesley Heights stop on the CityLYNX Gold Line opened August 30, 2021.
· The Irwin Creek, Stewart Creek and Wesley Heights greenway system has a 2.7-mile section from State Street to Cedar Street.
