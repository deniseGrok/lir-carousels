## Facts and sources (fetched and read Oct 28 2026)
A. NC REALTORS Q&A, The Offer to Purchase and Contract and Contingent Sales: the buyer's representation on Form 2-T that they must sell their current home "is merely information provided to the seller. It does not create a contingency"; a true contingent sale needs "an attorney-drafted, custom addendum"; otherwise the Due Diligence Period and Settlement Date "may be pushed out further than is typical"; the buyer "should try to close on their current property before the end of the Due Diligence Period, or at least be reasonably satisfied" it will close before settlement. https://www.ncrealtors.org/question/the-offer-to-purchase-and-contract-and-contingent-sales/ (slides 6, 7)
B. NC REALTORS Q&A, What are a buyer's rights under Form 2-T if they can't close on their existing property? (paragraph 5(b) is not a legal contingency). https://www.ncrealtors.org/question/what-are-a-buyers-rights-under-form-2-t-if-they-cant-close-on-their-existing-property/ (slide 6)
