## Title
Victorian Homes Inside Uptown Charlotte: Fourth Ward #Shorts

## Description
Turrets, slate roofs and fountains, right inside Uptown Charlotte.

Fourth Ward is one of the four wards Charlotte was divided into in the mid-1830s, about 30 city blocks today.
A 1976 Junior League restoration program brought its Queen Anne and Victorian Italianate homes back, starting with the 1884 Berryhill House.
Fourth Ward Park covers about three acres, with fountains, walking paths and ceramic birdhouses by artist Joan Bankemper.
Settlers' Cemetery, Charlotte's first municipal burial ground, was in use as early as 1776.

The market (Realtor.com, as of October 2026): median list price $345,000, median sold price $371,000, $408 per square foot, 100 active listings, 76 median days on market.
