# Neighborhood Spotlight: Ballantyne, Charlotte NC (Oct 15 2026, 4:00 PM ET slot)
10 slides, 1080x1350, nc_style teal/gold (Denise Oct 15 PM). Alternating light/dark. Tag: NEIGHBORHOOD SPOTLIGHT / CHARLOTTE NC. Photos: Wikimedia Commons, on-slide credit, NO Illustrative labels. Contact: left-aligned block (no email, no Carolinas). Trampled by Turtles Oct 17 CUT. Nothing posted.

## Slides
1. [Band: A CITY WITHIN THE CITY. Ballantyne.] South Charlotte's park-side town center: 535-acre campus of offices, parks, dining and live music; 100+ acres of green space, 20+ miles of paths; The Bowl, a new dining district on Bowl Street (A, B, H)
2. THE BOWL: The area's new living room. Dining, retail and entertainment at 15119 Bowl Street; garden rooms, sidewalk seating, rooftop decks; a central green; paths connect to Ballantyne's Backyard and the TD Amp (C, D)
3. PARKS: 100+ acres of backyard. Ballantyne's Backyard, a former golf course now a community park; trails, rolling hills, catch-and-release ponds; Stream Park, 6 acres with a playground, pavilion, stream, pond and hammocks (D, E)
4. TRAILS: 20+ miles of paths. Walking paths and bike lanes; The Fit Trail with fitness stations; trails around Hawkins and Crawford Ponds; Carolina Thread Trail via McAlpine Creek Greenway (A, B)
5. EAT: Dinner on Bowl Street. North Italia, Flower Child, Olde Mecklenburg Brewery, Postino, Brasserie Copain, Harriet's Hamburgers; plus Honeysuckle Gelato and PopUp Bagels (C, tenant list "Now Open + Coming Soon"; Coming Soon tenants excluded)
6. MORE FLAVOR: Tabla Indian Restaurant; Crispy Banh Mi (Vietnamese); Gallery Restaurant at The Ballantyne hotel (farm-fresh Southern); Hestia Rooftop (ice cream flights + cocktails); Fiore (1920s-style lounge) (B)
7. LIVE MUSIC: A stage in the park. TD Amp Ballantyne, 5,000-capacity outdoor venue; concerts, festivals, fitness classes; open daily to stroll when no event is on; on the calendar: Trampled by Turtles, Sat. Oct. 17 (F, H, I)
8. GETTING AROUND: Roads + transit. I-485 Express Lanes opened Feb. 2026 across south Charlotte; six new roads + intersection upgrades; CATS Route 43, eight stops on campus; three LYNX stops planned on campus (2030 transit plan) (G, J, K)
9. PRICES (Realtor.com, as of Sep 2026): Ballantyne West median list $418,750 / median sold $405,000; Ballantyne East median list $621,888 / median sold $657,000 (L, M)
10. MY RECOMMENDATION: Spend a Saturday here first.
