## LinkedIn
Neighborhood Spotlight: Wilmore, Charlotte NC. The City of Charlotte calls Wilmore Dilworth's sister neighborhood and one of Charlotte's first streetcar suburbs; City Council designated it a Local Historic District in 2010. Developer F. C. Abbott named it for two former owners, Wilson and Moore, and his company's 1914 plat set straight streets near the railroad and curving streets past Mint Street. The land includes the area around the Rudisill Gold Mine, one of the most productive mines of the country's first gold rush. Most buildings are Bungalow style, with low overhanging roofs, full-width front porches and Craftsman details. In a Local Historic District, exterior changes such as windows, doors, fencing and tree removal need a Certificate of Appropriateness from the Historic District Commission before work begins, so I recommend checking the design standards before you plan a renovation. Today the neighborhood has Wilmore Centennial Park at S. Tryon and Kingston, and LYNX Blue Line stations at Bland St. and East/West Blvd. Market snapshot (Realtor.com Economic Research, data through September 2026): $644,900 median list price, $444 list price per square foot, 18 homes for sale, 112 median days on market.
