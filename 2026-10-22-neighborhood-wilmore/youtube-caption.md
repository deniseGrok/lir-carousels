Title: Wilmore: Charlotte's Bungalow Neighborhood Next to Dilworth #Shorts

Description:
Bungalows, a 1914 street plan and gold-rush history, just across the rail line from Dilworth, southwest of Uptown Charlotte.
· Developer F. C. Abbott named Wilmore for two former owners, Wilson and Moore.
· The land includes the area around the Rudisill Gold Mine, one of the most productive mines of the country's first gold rush.
· Most buildings are Bungalow style, with low overhanging roofs, full-width front porches and Craftsman details.
· Wilmore has been a Charlotte Local Historic District since 2010, so exterior changes need Historic District Commission approval first.
· Wilmore Centennial Park sits at S. Tryon St. and Kingston Ave., and LYNX Blue Line stations sit at Bland St. and East/West Blvd.
