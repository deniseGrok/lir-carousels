🏡 Friends, if you're selling your home and closing day is getting close, this one's for you!

Here's what I walk my sellers through before they hand over the keys:

📅 Know exactly when the buyer takes possession under your contract
✍️ Need to stay a little longer after closing? Put it in a written agreement
📦 Take everything that isn't part of the sale, and leave the fixtures
🔑 Gather every key, code, and garage remote, and reset smart devices
💡 Coordinate utilities through the earlier of closing or buyer possession, and forward your mail

Planning your move-out can help protect your sale, your timeline, and your peace of mind. 💛
