## Slides (copy lives in build/build_slides.py)
1 Cover: "HANDING OVER" / script "the keys" / "Practical advice for SELLERS". Image gen-01.
2 Know your move-out date: on NC Form 2-T, possession, including keys, codes and garage openers, passes at Closing unless you agree otherwise; Closing ends when the deed is recorded. Bullets: Check your contract dates / Closing ends at recording / Plan your move around it / In SC, read your contract. Sources A ¶13, ¶1(o).
3 Need more time? Put it in writing: in NC, staying after closing uses a Seller Possession After Closing Agreement (Form 2A8-T); any change must be in writing and signed; in SC, put it in writing too. Bullets: Ask early, not at closing / Use the right form / Agree on dates and terms / Signed by all parties. Sources A ¶13, ¶19; B (Forms List: 2A8-T REV 7/2025 © 7/2026); C (S.C. Code 32-3-10, SC line is a recommendation).
4 Move out with care: Form 2-T expects the home in substantially the same or better condition at Closing as on the offer date, reasonable wear and tear excepted. Bullets: Protect floors and walls / Watch doorways and stairs / Note any moving damage / Tell your agent right away. Source A ¶11(a). Bullets are recommendations in Denise's voice.
5 Take it all, leave it clear: Form 2-T says to remove all personal property that isn't part of the sale, plus all garbage and debris, by the time possession is delivered. Bullets: Every closet and cabinet / Attic, garage and shed / Haul away trash and debris / Sweep up as you go. Source A ¶8(d). ("Broom-clean" is NOT 2-T wording and is not used; "Sweep up as you go" is a courtesy recommendation.)
6 Fixtures stay, so do the keys: fixtures in the sale stay put; hand over keys, mailbox keys, codes and garage openers, and unpair smart devices before Closing. Bullets: Every key and code / Garage door openers / Unpair and reset devices / Leave manuals behind. Sources A ¶2(a), ¶2(c), ¶13. "Leave manuals behind" is a courtesy recommendation (not a contract term).
7 Keep utilities on to the end: Form 2-T has the seller keep existing utilities on, at the seller's cost, through the earlier of Closing or buyer possession, so inspections and the walk-through can happen. Bullets: Call each provider early / Coordinate the stop date / Keep power and water on / Watch for final bills. Sources A ¶8(c); E, F (providers take stop/move requests). Bullets are recommendations.
8 Forward your mail: submit a USPS change of address before you move; banks and government agencies may not let sensitive mail forward, so update them directly. Bullets: File with USPS early / Tell banks and agencies / Update your accounts / Leave the mailbox key. Sources G (USPS), A ¶13 (mailbox keys).
9 One last look before you go: the buyer may do a final walk-through before closing; do your own last pass, and keep your homeowners insurance until the deed is recorded. Bullets: Walk every room / Lock windows and doors / Leave keys as agreed / Insure until recorded. Sources A ¶8(c) (final walk-through access), ¶11(b) (Seller advised not to cancel insurance until after confirming recordation of the deed), D (CFPB walk-through before signing).

## Facts and sources (all verified live Wed Nov 11 2026, ~4:30-4:50 AM ET)
A. NC REALTORS / NC Bar Standard Form 2-T, Offer to Purchase and Contract. Current version REV 8/2026 (B); the REV 8/2026 file is member-only, so text was verified on the public July 2026 marked-up form https://www.ncrealtors.org/wp-content/uploads/markedup0726-2-T.pdf (downloaded Nov 11 2026). Per NC REALTORS the 8/2026 revision adds a Form 220 checkbox in ¶14 (re-checked Nov 10 2026), not a change to the paragraphs below.
 - ¶1(o) "Closing": completion of the legal process transferring title, including Settlement, title update, authorization to disburse, and "recordation in the appropriate county registry of the deed(s)".
 - ¶2(a): "ALL EXISTING FIXTURES ARE INCLUDED IN THE SALE AS PART OF THE PURCHASE PRICE, FREE OF LIENS, UNLESS EXCLUDED".
 - ¶2(c): prior to Closing, Seller shall unpair devices that convey, delete personal data, and restore them to factory default settings unless otherwise agreed.
 - ¶8(c): Seller provides reasonable access through the earlier of Closing or possession by Buyer, including for a final walk-through inspection; "Seller's obligation includes providing existing utilities operating at Seller's cost".
 - ¶8(d): "Seller shall remove, by the date possession is made available to Buyer, all personal property which is not a part of the purchase and all garbage and debris from the Property."
 - ¶11(a): if the Property is not in substantially the same or better condition at Closing as on the date of the offer, reasonable wear and tear excepted, Buyer may terminate.
 - ¶11(b): risk of loss before Closing is on Seller; "Seller is advised not to cancel existing insurance on the Property until after confirming recordation of the deed."
 - ¶13: Possession, including all means of access (keys including mailbox keys, codes including security codes, garage door openers, electronic devices, etc.), delivered upon Closing unless otherwise provided: Buyer Possession Before Closing Agreement (Form 2A7-T) / Seller Possession After Closing Agreement (Form 2A8-T).
 - ¶19: "All changes, additions or deletions hereto must be in writing and signed by all parties."
B. NC REALTORS Forms List "As of October 19, 2026" https://www.ncrealtors.org/wp-content/uploads/formslist.pdf (downloaded Nov 11 2026): 2-T REV 8/2026; 2A7-T Buyer Possession Before Closing Agreement (REV 7/2025, © 7/2026); 2A8-T Seller Possession After Closing Agreement (REV 7/2025, © 7/2026).
C. S.C. Code §32-3-10 (Statute of Frauds), official South Carolina Legislature https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t32c003.php (read Nov 11 2026): contracts for the sale of lands must be in writing and signed. Slides 2-3 SC lines are recommendations ("read your contract", "put it in writing too"); SC REALTORS forms are member-only and were not reviewed, so no SC form names are used.
D. CFPB, "Close the deal" https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/close/close-the-deal/ (read Nov 11 2026): before signing, the buyer does a final walk-through to confirm agreed repairs and that items the seller agreed to leave are in place.
E. Duke Energy, "Start, Stop or Move Service" https://www.duke-energy.com/home/start-stop-move (read Nov 11 2026): Stop My Service / Move Service to New Address options.
F. Charlotte Water, "Moving? Need to start or stop water service?" https://www.charlottenc.gov/water/Customer-Care/Start-Stop-Service (Nov 11 2026): call 311 or 704-336-7600 and ask for "water move in or move out". (Provider names are not on the slides; used only to support "call each provider".)
G. USPS, "Standard Forward Mail & Change of Address" https://www.usps.com/manage/forward.htm (read Nov 11 2026): submit a change of address when moving; government agencies and financial institutions do not allow forwarding of sensitive documents; contact senders directly; you are responsible for telling agencies and companies.
