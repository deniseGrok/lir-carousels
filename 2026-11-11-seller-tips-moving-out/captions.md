## Instagram
🏡 Your home is sold… now what happens when you hand over the keys?

Before moving day, it helps to know what your contract expects from you between now and closing.

📅 Do you know when the buyer takes possession?
✍️ If you need more time, is it in a written agreement?
📦 Are you taking only what isn't part of the sale?
🔑 Is every key, code, and garage remote ready to hand over?
💡 Are utilities and access arranged through the earlier of closing or buyer possession?

A smooth move-out protects your sale, your timeline, and your peace of mind.

Packing is the easy part. Following your contract is what gets you to the closing table.

## Facebook personal profile
🏡 Friends, if you're selling your home and closing day is getting close, this one's for you!

Here's what I walk my sellers through before they hand over the keys:

📅 Know exactly when the buyer takes possession under your contract
✍️ Need to stay a little longer after closing? Put it in a written agreement
📦 Take everything that isn't part of the sale, and leave the fixtures
🔑 Gather every key, code, and garage remote, and reset smart devices
💡 Coordinate utilities through the earlier of closing or buyer possession, and forward your mail

Planning your move-out can help protect your sale, your timeline, and your peace of mind. 💛
