## Instagram
🏡 Your buyer asked for help… credit or price cut?

Before you answer, it helps to know how each one changes the deal.

💰 Does a credit help with closing costs, or does a cut change the sale price?
📊 Have you compared your net with both options?
📄 Do you know the lender's cap on seller contributions?
✍️ Will the change be in a signed written agreement?
🏠 How might an appraisal treat a credit differently from a cut?

These choices shape your net proceeds and the buyer's loan.

A credit and a cut are not the same tool. Compare both before you choose.
