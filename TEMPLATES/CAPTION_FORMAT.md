# Caption format (approved by Denise Wilson, Oct 31 2026)

Applies to all three daily carousels (New Construction, Buyer/Seller Tips, Neighborhood Spotlight). Replaces the Oct 22 format
(the old format had no phone number and a closing question). Write plain text only: no markdown, no asterisks, no bold.
Tailor the caption to each platform, but keep the same structure and voice.

## Structure (in order, with one blank line between blocks)
1. Hook: one emoji and one line, usually a question that names the reader's situation.
   e.g. "🏡 You may love the house… but do you know exactly what you're buying?"
2. Setup: one sentence on why this matters before they act.
3. Checklist: 4-5 short lines, each starting with its own topic emoji (🔑 📍 📄 🏘️ 🛡️ 💰 📅 🧰 🌳 🚗 …). Usually questions.
   Match the slide topics. Do not include stats or numbers, which belong on the sourced stats slide.
4. Why it matters: one sentence (rights, plans, money).
5. Two-beat line (optional): e.g. "A beautiful home is exciting. An informed decision is essential."
6. Save/swipe line: "👉 Swipe through these tips and save this post before your next home purchase." (see TikTok note).
7. CTA: "💬 Thinking about buying or selling in the Charlotte Metro, NC or SC? Let's connect and discuss your next move!"
8. Signature, three lines:
   Denise Wilson | Broker/REALTOR®
   Lifestyle International Realty — Carolinas
   📲 704-615-7347
9. Blank line, then the hashtags on one line.

## Full example (Denise's)
🏡 You may love the house… but do you know exactly what you're buying?

Before you sign on the dotted line, there are important details every homebuyer should understand.

These details can affect your property rights, future plans, and financial investment.

## Per platform
- Instagram (4:5 via Buffer): the full format. Up to 5 hashtags. No email address and no links (IG captions do not link).
- Facebook Page (1:1 via Buffer): the full format, slightly tighter (it can drop the two-beat line). CTA can say "Send me a message or call".
  2-3 hashtags.
- TikTok (9:16 via Buffer): the shortest version. Hook, 3-4 checklist lines as short fragments, a save line, a one-line CTA, the signature,
  and 3-4 hashtags. Do not use "swipe" on TikTok (compliance_check.py flags it for 9:16 and video); use "Save this…" instead.
- Facebook personal profile (Denise posts it herself; this is the only caption delivered in chat): warmer and first person, e.g. opening with
  "Friends, if you're house hunting, this one's for you!" and "here's what I walk my buyers through". Use "share this with anyone…" as the
  save line, a friendly CTA ("Message me anytime"), the same signature, and 1-2 hashtags.
- Seller tips: use the same structure with seller topics (pricing prep, repairs, disclosures, showings, offers). New Construction and
  Neighborhood Spotlight: use the same structure with checklist lines about the community or features (no people or demographics).
- Spotlight YouTube Short (if any): keep the silent .mp4 rule and the same signature. Do not use "swipe".

## Compliance (TEMPLATES/COMPLIANCE.md; NC and SC Real Estate Commissions, Fair Housing)
- The firm name "Lifestyle International Realty — Carolinas" and the phone are always in the signature. Equal Housing Opportunity is on slide 10.
- No guarantees or promised outcomes ("will sell", "guaranteed", "best/#1"), and no urgency ("act now", "last chance").
- No demographic or steering language: never describe who a home or area suits ("perfect for families", "safe", "family-friendly", "exclusive",
  schools, crime, religion, disability). Describe homes, features, documents and process only.
- No statistics in captions (numbers stay on the sourced slide). Never check or state license numbers.
- Expected compliance_check.py FAIL lines on captions under this format: "Equal Housing Opportunity" (it is on slide 10), "ends with a question"
  (the new format ends with the signature and hashtags), and "Swipe" on IG/FB captions. Treat these as approved overrides.
