## Truthful advertising (NCREC + SCREC)
| # | Check | Pass when | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | No misleading or unverifiable claims | Every fact is verified on a primary source TODAY or cut. No "best", "#1", "top agent", "guaranteed sale", "sell in X days", "highest price". | G.S. 93A-6(a)(1)-(3) (misrepresentation, false promises, course of misrepresentation through advertising); S.C. Code 40-57-710(A)(2)-(4) |
| T2 | No guarantees | Never promise a price, profit, appreciation, timeline, approval or outcome ("will sell", "you'll net", "values will rise"). Use "can", "may", "ask", "I recommend". | S.C. Code 40-57-710(A)(3), (A)(7) (guaranteeing future profits); G.S. 93A-6(a)(2) |
| T3 | No fake urgency | No "act now", "prices will never be this low", "last chance", invented deadlines. Real dated deadlines only (e.g., a builder's stated end date). | G.S. 93A-6(a)(2); S.C. Code 40-57-710(A)(3) |
| T4 | Numbers carry source + date | Every number on a slide or in a caption names its source and period (e.g., "Realtor.com, Sep 2026"). Source line on the stats slide; full citations in the .md. | T1 |
| T5 | Photos | No photo is presented as a specific listing, address or named place unless it really is. Generic photos get generic tags (topic words), never an address or a named home. No visible house numbers or readable plates. | G.S. 93A-6(a)(1); 21 NCAC 58A .0105(a)(2) (no advertising someone's property without the owner's written consent); S.C. Code 40-57-135(E)(1) (no advertising another's property without a written listing agreement / listing firm's authorization) |
| T6 | Listings of other firms | Never advertise a specific property listed by another firm without written authorization and a clear credit to the listing firm. Default: don't feature specific listings at all. | S.C. Code 40-57-135(E)(1); 21 NCAC 58A .0105(a)(2) |
| T7 | Builder incentives | Labeled as **the builder's offer**, with the date verified on the builder's site, and "terms and eligibility set by the builder; subject to change". Never imply Denise or the firm is offering it. | T1; G.S. 93A-6(a)(1) |
| T8 | Legal / tax / lending | Explain what a rule or form says and point to the professional ("talk with a real estate attorney / tax professional / your lender"). Never give it as a guarantee or personal advice ("you won't owe tax", "you'll qualify"). Add "Not legal or tax advice." in the .md and YouTube description when the post covers law or tax. | G.S. 93A-6(a)(1)-(2); S.C. Code 40-57-350/-710 scope of expertise |
| T9 | Mortgage trigger terms | If a slide or caption states a down payment amount/percent, number of payments/term, payment amount, or finance charge for a loan, it must also give down payment, repayment terms and the APR — so default is: don't state them. | 12 CFR 1026.24(d) (Regulation Z) |

## Fair Housing (federal, NC, SC, Charlotte)
Protected classes: race, color, religion, sex, handicap/disability, familial status, national origin (42 U.S.C. 3604(c); 24 CFR 100.75; N.C.G.S. 41A-4(a)(6) "handicapping condition"; S.C. Code 31-21-40(3); Charlotte Fair Housing Ordinance, which the City says covers race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status and disability). Advertising may not indicate any preference, limitation or discrimination, by words, photos, illustrations or symbols (24 CFR 100.75(c)(1)).
| # | Check | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Describe property and places, never people | Talk about the home (bedrooms, porch, kitchen), the land, streets, parks, trails, transit, distances, events. Never who lives there, who it suits, or who "belongs". |
| F2 | Banned words/phrases | None of: "perfect for" (anyone), "ideal for", "great for families/couples/singles/retirees/empty nesters/young professionals", "family-friendly", "kid-friendly", "safe", "safe neighborhood", "low crime", "crime", "exclusive", "private community" used as a people signal, "integrated", "diverse", "ethnic", "Christian/church community", "walking distance to [named church/temple/mosque]" as a selling point, "handicap"/"no wheelchairs", "able-bodied", "bachelor pad", "man cave", "mother-in-law" (use "guest suite"), "master" (use "primary"), "near good schools", "top-rated schools", school ratings or school quality. |
| F3 | Schools and crime | No school quality, ratings or assignment claims; no crime or safety claims. If asked, point people to the school district and police data directly. |
| F4 | People in photos | No people as the focus; never images that suggest who should live there. |
| F5 | Steering | Never suggest an area fits or doesn't fit a group, or that a group is moving in/out (blockbusting, 42 U.S.C. 3604(e); S.C. Code 31-21-40(5)). |
| F6 | EHO statement | R4 is present. |
HUD's Jan 9, 1995 advertising memo (Achtenberg, "Guidance Regarding Advertisements Under §804(c)") treats neutral property descriptions ("family room", "quiet streets", "jogging trails", "walk-in closets") as facially neutral; this checklist is intentionally stricter than that memo.
