Business in Mint Hill

Doing Business In Mint HillMint Hill, North Carolina is the perfect setting for business owners and professionals seeking a prosperous, vibrant, thriving community for doing business.  Our mayor and commissioners are very proud of the quality of life in Mint Hill.  In addition to the economic development opportunities, there are numerous cultural and recreational opportunities that make Mint Hill the best choice for new industries.
