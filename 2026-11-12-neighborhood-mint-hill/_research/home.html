Message from Mayor Dale Dalton

Mint Hill Residents,

This Veterans Day let’s come together as a community to honor those who have defended our nation. May we always remember their sacrifices and those made by their families and loved ones. To every veteran in Mint Hill and beyond, thank you—your service will never be forgotten.

Our Hometown Hero banners are proudly on display around town to highlight the incredible heroes right here in our community. Veterans are a treasured part of Mint Hill—they are our parents, grandparents, brothers, sisters, sons, and daughters. We should honor them every day for protecting the freedom we too often take for granted. If you would like to nominate someone for a banner, please visit our website.

On behalf of the Town of Mint Hill Board of Commissioners, thank you for defending our freedom, liberty, and American way of life. I also want to express our deepest gratitude to veterans’ families, who have endured difficult times and carried the responsibility of keeping the home fires burning, as well as our local veteran organizations for all they do for our hometown heroes. The next time you see a veteran, please join us in thanking them for helping make Mint Hill such a wonderful place to live and raise a family.

The Board of Commissioners will meet on Thursday, November 12, at 7:00 p.m. at Mint Hill Town Hall. A full agenda will be available on our website later this week.

God bless our veterans and troops. God bless Mint Hill, and God bless America!

Dale Dalton
Mint Hill Mayor

Mint Hill Community Center

The Mint Hill Community Center is dedicated to enriching the lives of all residents by providing an inclusive and welcoming environment for recreation, learning, and connection. Our mission is to foster community engagement through diverse programs and accessible spaces that promote wellness, creativity, and a sense of belonging for every generation.
