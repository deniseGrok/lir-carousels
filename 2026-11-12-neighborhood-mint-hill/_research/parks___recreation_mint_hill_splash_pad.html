Mint Hill Splash Pad


This exciting new attraction is conveniently located at 11524 Bain School Road. The splash pad promises to be a wonderful addition to the community, offering a refreshing and fun-filled experience for families and children of all ages. This new facility is designed to provide a safe and interactive environment where visitors can enjoy a variety of water features, including sprinklers, fountains, and playful water jets. Perfect for family fun, offering a wide array of inclusive features to ensure everyone can join in on the adventure. Enjoy the refreshing water play and make unforgettable summer memories.
