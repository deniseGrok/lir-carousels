Nature Centers and Preserves

Mecklenburg County manages over 8,300-acres of nature preserves throughout the county. We offer four nature centers along with a nature space at Eastway Regional Recreation Center. Each of these locations offers environmental education programming and general visitor information.

  • McDowell Nature Center and Preserve

    15222 York Road, Charlotte, NC, 28278

  • Natural Resources

  • Nature Preserves

  • Quest at Latta Nature Preserve

    6345 Sample Road, Huntersville, NC, 28078

  • Reedy Creek Nature Center and Preserve

    2986 Rocky River Road, Charlotte, NC, 28215

  • Stevens Creek Nature Center and Preserve

    15700 Thompson Road, Mint Hill, NC, 28227
