## Facebook personal profile
🏡 Friends, if you're buying a home and the inspection report just landed in your inbox, this one's for you!

Here's what I walk my buyers through before we send a repair request:

📋 Read the whole report, and get a repair pro's opinion on the big items
⏰ Send requests early, so there's time to negotiate before your deadline
✍️ Spell out what gets fixed, how, and by whom, and get it in writing
💰 Weigh a repair, a credit, or a price change, and check with your lender first
🔍 Verify the work before closing, at a re-inspection or the walk-through

Asking is part of the process. Getting it in writing is your protection. 💛
