## Facts and sources (verified live Sun Nov 8 2026, ~4:15–4:45 AM ET)
A. NC REALTORS / NC Bar Standard Form 2-T, Offer to Purchase and Contract. Current version REV 8/2026 (NC REALTORS Forms List as of Oct 19, 2026, https://www.ncrealtors.org/wp-content/uploads/formslist.pdf); the REV 8/2026 file is member-only, so text was verified on the public July 2026 marked-up form https://www.ncrealtors.org/wp-content/uploads/markedup0726-2-T.pdf. Per NC REALTORS, the 8/2026 revision adds a Form 220 checkbox in ¶14 (https://www.ncrealtors.org/question/is-form-220-part-of-the-contract-if-the-checkbox-in-paragraph-14-of-form-2-t-is-not-checked/), and the 2026 changes summary lists no change to the repair paragraphs (https://www.ncrealtors.org/question/summary-of-2026-changes-to-nc-realtors-residential-forms/).
 - ¶1(i): DD Fee is the property of Seller upon the Effective Date and a credit to Buyer at Closing.
 - ¶4 (intro): buyer not satisfied should terminate before DD Period expires unless a written extension is obtained; Seller is not obligated to grant an extension; terminating outside the DD Period, buyer may lose the Earnest Money Deposit.
 - ¶4(d): "THE PROPERTY IS BEING SOLD IN ITS CURRENT CONDITION" unless the parties agree otherwise; Seller may, but is not required to, negotiate repairs/improvements; Buyer strongly advised to make requests in sufficient time to conclude negotiations before the DD Period expires; any repair agreement is an addition to the Contract that must be in writing and signed by the parties per ¶19.
 - ¶4(g): Buyer may terminate for any reason or no reason by written Termination Notice during the DD Period (or agreed written extension), time is of the essence; Earnest Money Deposit refunded.
 - ¶5(a): some loans and programs may impose repair obligations or additional conditions or costs on Seller or Buyer.
 - ¶8(c): Seller provides reasonable access to verify satisfactory completion of negotiated repairs and for the final walk-through.
 - ¶8(m): negotiated repairs made in a good and workmanlike manner; Buyer has the right to verify before Settlement.
 - ¶19: all changes, additions or deletions must be in writing and signed by all parties.
B. NCREC Bulletin, "Due Diligence" Questions and Answers (Feb 2014, Vol. 44-3): https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/due-diligence-questions-and-answers/ — buyer may ask for any number of repairs; seller not obligated to agree; repairs completely negotiable; if unsatisfied, buyer can terminate or move forward without the repairs; repairs completed in a good and workmanlike manner before settlement; buyer may verify repairs during or after DD; termination is unilateral, in writing, during DD; buyer typically gets back earnest money but not the DD fee unless otherwise negotiated; negotiate enough DD time for repairs.
C. NCREC Bulletin, "Avoid Vaguely Written DDRA's" (July 2019 e-bulletin, Sheryl B. Graham): https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/avoid-vaguely-written-ddras/ — Form 310-T Due Diligence Request and Agreement; language must be clear and specific: who will perform repairs, what will be repaired, how; an inspection item number alone is not enough; consider input from a repair professional; recommend re-inspection if desired; ratifying the DDRA does not end the DD period; use properly prepared documentation for money in lieu of repairs or DD period changes. (Form 310-T is REV 7/2020 © 7/2026 per the Forms List.)
D. CFPB, "Schedule a home inspection": https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/close/schedule-a-home-inspection/ — schedule ASAP to leave time to resolve problems; you may negotiate who makes or pays for repairs; seller may or may not agree depending on contract and market; you may ask the seller to fix items or give a credit for the cost of repairs; lender can require major repairs before closing.
E. CFPB, "Close the deal": https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/close/close-the-deal/ — final walk-through to confirm agreed repairs before signing; seller credits toward closing costs instead of a repair; don't sign until documents match expectations.
F. S.C. Code §32-3-10 (Statute of Frauds): contracts for the sale of lands must be in writing and signed by the party to be charged — https://law.justia.com/codes/south-carolina/title-32/chapter-3/section-32-3-10/ (scstatehouse.gov blocked the fetch today). Slide 5 SC line is a recommendation ("put it in writing too"), not a claim about SC repair forms (SC REALTORS forms are member-only and were not reviewed).
G. NC REALTORS / NC Bar Form 4-T, Agreement to Amend Contract (REV 7/2026), marked-up public copy https://www.ncrealtors.org/wp-content/uploads/markedup0726-4-T.pdf — checkboxes to change the Purchase Price, the Due Diligence Fee, and the DD Period expiration.

Realtor.com Economic Research, metro core metrics CSV https://econdata.s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/Reports/Core/RDC_Inventory_Core_Metrics_Metro.csv (Last-Modified Nov 4, 2026 11:56 AM ET; re-downloaded Nov 8 ~4:30 AM ET), row 202610 / CBSA 16740 Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia NC-SC: median_days_on_market 65 (yy +0.1111 → +11.1%), active_listing_count 11,547 (yy +0.1321 → +13.2%), pending_listing_count 4,129 (yy −0.1299 → −13.0%), price_reduced_share 0.2714 → 27.1% (yy +0.0004 → +0.0 pts).
