## 3. Market data (slide 2): Realtor.com
- **Page:** Realtor.com, "Elizabeth in Charlotte, NC housing & rental market overview", https://www.realtor.com/local/market/north-carolina/charlotte/elizabeth
- **Checked:** Oct 17, 2026, about 5:17 PM ET. I opened the page directly and read the "market summary" table.
- **Exact lines I read:**
  - "Key indicators as of October 2026"
  - "Median listing $ | $701,000"
  - "Median sold $ | $870,000"
  - "$ per sq ft | $346/sq ft"
  - "Active listings | 40"
  - "Median days on market | 71 days"
  - The page credits "Source: Realtor.com® Economic Research".
- **On the slide:** MEDIAN LIST $701,000 · MEDIAN SOLD $870,000 · PER SQ FT $346 (40 active listings) · DAYS ON MKT 71 (median) · "REALTOR.COM · AS OF OCTOBER 2026" · "Source: Realtor.com Economic Research, Elizabeth"
- **Left out, and why:**
  - The month-over-month and year-over-year percentages (e.g. "-14.30%" on list price): they swing month to month and would read as urgency.
  - Rent figures ($1,871/mo, 112 rentals): they're off-topic for a home-buying slide.
  - Realtor.com's own commentary ("buyer's market", "strong entry point"): that's opinion, not data.
  - The school ratings table: Fair Housing. We never steer by schools.
- **Images:** `03-realtor-elizabeth-page.png` (the page) and `03b-realtor-market-summary-zoom.png` (the table, zoomed).

## 4. Leads only: Charlotte's Got a Lot and Wikipedia
- **Charlotte's Got a Lot** (Charlotte's official tourism site): https://www.charlottesgotalot.com/neighborhoods/elizabeth, opened directly about 5:18 PM ET.
  - I used it as a list of leads: Independence Park, Lupie's Cafe, The Stanley, Visulite Theatre, The Crunkleton, Earl's Grocery and Elizabeth Creamery.
  - It describes Independence Park as "19 acres of green space toward the park's reflecting pond and rose garden".
  - Image: `05-cgal-elizabeth-page.png`
- **Wikipedia:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_(Charlotte_neighborhood), opened directly about 5:19 PM ET.
  - It gives "Added to NRHP: January 3, 1989" and "NRHP reference No. 88003003".
  - Wikipedia is never the final source, so I took those two numbers to the official records (step 5).
  - I skipped its population figures (Fair Housing) and its "second oldest streetcar suburb" line (it rests on a secondary essay, and a 2-slide demo didn't need it).

## 5. Checked the cover fact (slide 1) against official records
Slide 1 fact: "The Elizabeth Historic District joined the National Register of Historic Places on January 3, 1989."
- **NPGallery (National Park Service):**
  - Page: "Elizabeth Historic District", https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/88003003, checked about 5:20 PM ET.
  - Exact lines: "National Register Information System ID: 88003003", "Architectural Styles: COLONIAL REVIVAL BUNGALOW/CRAFTSMAN TUDOR REVIVAL", "Date Published: 1/3/1989"
  - Image: `06-npgallery-nrhp-88003003.png`
- **National Park Service, "National Register of Historic Places 1989 Weekly Lists" (Jan–June):**
  - Page: https://www.nps.gov/subjects/nationalregister/upload/weekly-list-1989-Jan-June-national-register-of-historic-places.pdf, found with search 2, checked about 5:22 PM ET.
  - Page 10 reads: "NORTH CAROLINA / Mecklenburg county / Elizabeth Historic District … Charlotte 1/03/89 88003003". This is the official listing date.
  - Image: `06d-nps-weekly-list-1989-p10-010.png`
- **NC State Historic Preservation Office, National Register nomination MK0866:**
  - Page: https://files.nc.gov/ncdcr/nr/MK0866.pdf, found with search 1, checked about 5:20 PM ET.
  - Exact lines: "The Elizabeth Historic District is an irregularly configured suburban residential neighborhood comprising approximately 265 acres". It also says Dilworth and Myers Park "were listed in the National Register in 1987; with the Elizabeth Historic District, they ring Charlotte on the south and east".
  - Image: `06b-nrhp-nomination-p1-001.png` (cover page)
- **Result:** three official sources agree on Jan 3, 1989 and reference number 88003003, so the fact went on the slide.

## 6. Researched but not used (only one cover fact fits a 2-slide demo)
- **Independence Park:**
  - Sources: the Mecklenburg County news release "Charlotte's Oldest Public Park Reopens Following $6M Renovation" (June 12, 2023), https://news.mecknc.gov/news/charlottes-oldest-public-park-reopens-following-6m-renovation. And the county's Historic Landmarks page, https://hl.mecknc.gov/Properties/Designated-Historic-Landmarks/charlotte/east-inner/independence-park. Both checked about 5:19 PM ET.
  - Verified lines: "Independence Park, the first and oldest public park in Charlotte" and "The 34-acre park in Charlotte's Elizabeth neighborhood". The landmarks page says John Nolen was selected "to design the park".
  - **Conflict found:** Charlotte's Got a Lot says "19 acres" and a "rose garden". The county says 34 acres, and its Historic Landmarks page says the 1931 Sunnyside Rose Garden "was destroyed by the construction of Independence Boulevard (1949-1950) and the Brookshire Freeway (1960)". On a full carousel I would use the county figures and cut both tourism-site claims.
  - Image: `07-meck-county-independence-park.png`
- **Gold Line streetcar:** CATS page "Gold Line Streetcar", https://www.charlottenc.gov/CATS/Ride/Rail/Gold-Line-Streetcar, checked about 5:21 PM ET. It reads "The 4-mile-long streetcar line has 17 stops and connects the Historic West End through Center City Charlotte to the Elizabeth neighborhood." Verified, but not needed for 2 slides.
- **Willow oaks:** the NC HPO nomination (page 6) says developers planted oaks, and "willow oaks… now create a green canopy… most notably on Clement Avenue and the appropriately named Greenway Avenue". I drafted the cover around this first, then switched to the National Register fact. The best crisp photo was a porch, not a canopy street, and the copy must match the picture.
- **Restaurants and venues** (Lupie's, The Stanley, Visulite, The Crunkleton): I didn't check them on their own websites today, so they're not on the slides. They would need that check before going on a full carousel.

## 7. Fair Housing check
The slides describe places, prices and amenities only. I left out:
- Wikipedia's population figures
- Charlotte's Got a Lot's lines about the people found on the main strip ("long-time Charlotteans… students")
- Realtor.com's school ratings
- the county's quote of the park's 1905 deed language (true history, but it's about people and not needed here)

## 8. Choosing the photos
Requirement: crisp, scroll-stopping, free license, never misidentify a place. Image: `08-photo-candidates.png`
**Real Elizabeth photos (Wikimedia Commons), rejected:** I searched Wikimedia Commons for Elizabeth and Independence Park photos.
- "Lamar and Eighth in Elizabeth.jpg" (public domain): a snapshot with road signs and trash bins.
- The Independence Park photos by City Dweller 2 (CC BY-SA 4.0): phone snapshots, soft at full size, and one has litter on the lawn.
- I didn't use either, because the PHOTO HABIT rejects snapshot-quality images.

**Pexels candidates:** found with web searches such as "pexels.com photo craftsman bungalow house front porch exterior sunny" and "pexels.com photo sunny residential street oak trees canopy houses sidewalk summer Southern neighborhood".
- Rejected Pexels 34625609 (craftsman house): grey overcast sky and a window AC unit.
- Rejected Pexels 31570478 (street): a bare lot with utility poles.
- **Picked for slide 1:** Pexels 5353939, "American Bungalow House with a Flag Attached to a Porch" by Curtis Adams. https://www.pexels.com/photo/american-bungalow-house-with-a-flag-attached-to-a-porch-5353939/ Original is 4000x2667. It's a sunny bungalow porch under a blue sky, matching the Bungalow/Craftsman styles listed for the district. Image: `09-pexels-5353939-slide1-photo.png`
- **Picked for slide 2:** Pexels 37860098, "Charming American Suburban Street in Summer" by Clinton Weaver (Mount Gretna, PA). https://www.pexels.com/photo/charming-american-suburban-street-in-summer-37860098/ Original is 3024x4032. Lush porches under trees, and it stays readable behind the strong teal fade. Image: `10-pexels-37860098-slide2-photo.png`
- **First draft swapped:** I first had the porch-street photo on the cover and a craftsman house on the stats slide. I swapped because the craftsman house was weak (grey sky, AC unit).
- **License:** Pexels License, free for commercial use with no attribution required, so there's no credit on the slides.
- **Honest labeling:** neither photo shows Elizabeth, and neither is labeled as a specific place. They show the home style and the feel, not a specific address.

## 9. Full-size photo check
- **What I did:** viewed a 100% crop of each original before resizing (`11-photo-full-size-check.png`). Both are sharp.

## 10. Building the slides
Scripts: `build/prep_photos.py` (photos) and `build/build_slides.py` (slides). Both use the saved `carousel_style.py` with theme `fullbleed_teal`, the same as Myers Park.
- **Slide 1:**
  - Tag "ELIZABETH"
  - Gold band "CHARLOTTE NC. ELIZABETH."
  - Headline "A historic district."
  - One fact line: "The Elizabeth Historic District joined the National Register of Historic Places on January 3, 1989"
- **Slide 2:**
  - Tag "THE MARKET"
  - Headline "What homes cost here."
  - Strong teal fade over the photo
  - Four Realtor.com rows
  - The only source line in the demo
- **Footer and numbering:** footer "NEIGHBORHOOD SPOTLIGHT"; page numbers read 1 / 2 and 2 / 2.
- **Rules:** no text under 30px (the template enforces this) and no "Illustrative image" labels.
- **Not included:** no contact block, since this 2-slide demo has no slide 10. A full carousel puts it on slide 10.
- **First draft fixed:** the cover headline "Historic homes, listed since 1989." wrapped badly ("1989." alone on a line). I shortened it to "A historic district."
