## Description
A century-old oak canopy, just south of Uptown Charlotte.

Myers Park was planned in the early 1900s by John Nolen and Earle Sumner Draper.
It's a historic district on the National Register of Historic Places, with 1920s Colonial Revival and Tudor Revival homes.
Walk the 2.85-mile Booty Loop, stroll The Duke Mansion's 4.5 acres of gardens, or visit Wing Haven's three gardens.
Little Sugar Creek Greenway runs along the western edge, right next to Freedom Park.

The market (Realtor.com, as of October 2026): median list price $2,111,250, median sold price $1,276,500, $530 per square foot, 90 active listings, 50 median days on market.
