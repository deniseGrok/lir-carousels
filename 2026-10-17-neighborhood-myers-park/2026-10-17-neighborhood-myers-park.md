## Slides
1. OAK CANOPY AND HISTORIC HOMES. MYERS PARK. (hook band: CHARLOTTE NC. MYERS PARK.) Just south of Uptown Charlotte; planned in the early 1900s on former cotton farmland; laid out by planners John Nolen and Earle Sumner Draper (A, B)
2. HOMES WITH A HISTORY. 1920s Colonial Revival and Tudor Revival homes; Bungalow and Craftsman styles too; a historic district on the National Register of Historic Places (A, B)
3. STREETS UNDER THE OAKS. Oak canopy more than 100 years old; mansions and gardens along Queens Road West; the 2.85-mile Booty Loop for walking, running and cycling (A, B)
4. A HISTORIC ESTATE. The Duke Mansion: listed on the National Register; 20 individually decorated guest rooms; 4.5 acres of gardens, free to stroll during daylight hours (C)
5. WING HAVEN'S THREE GARDENS. Wing Haven Garden & Bird Sanctuary; Elizabeth Lawrence House & Garden; SEED Wildlife & Children's Garden; on Ridgewood Avenue, open Wednesday to Saturday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. (D, E, B)
6. GREENWAY AT THE EDGE. Little Sugar Creek Greenway along the western edge; right next to Freedom Park; tree-lined, walkable streets (B, A)
7. PORCHES, PLATES AND POURS. Selwyn Avenue Pub, a Myers Park porch since 1990; Napa on Providence on Perrin Place; Dot Dot Dot speakeasy at nearby Park Road Shopping Center (F, G, H, A)
8. CLOSE TO IT ALL. Uptown just to the north; main routes Providence Road, Kings Drive and Queens Road; Theatre Charlotte on Queens Road; Mint Museum Randolph nearby (B, I, J, A)
9. WHAT HOMES COST HERE (Realtor.com, as of October 2026): median list $2,111,250; median sold $1,276,500; $530/sq ft; 90 active listings; median 50 days on market (K)
