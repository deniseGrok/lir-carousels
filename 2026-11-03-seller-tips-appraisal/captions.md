## Instagram
🏡 Your buyer said yes… now the appraiser comes to visit.

If your buyer is getting a loan, their lender may require an appraisal, and a little prep helps.

📋 Do you have a list of your upgrades and when you made them?
🔑 Can the appraiser reach the attic, crawl space, gates and sheds?
🔧 Are the small repairs finished?
🤝 Do you know what you can share, and what you shouldn't say?
📉 Do you have a plan if the value comes in low?

The appraisal can shape the next steps in your sale, so it helps to be ready for it.

You can't set the value. You can make the facts easy to see.

🏡 Friends, if you're thinking about selling your home, this one's for you!

Once you accept an offer from a buyer who's getting a loan, their lender may require an appraisal of your home.

Here's what I encourage my sellers to have ready:

📋 A list of your upgrades and when you made them
🔑 Easy access to the attic, crawl space, gates and sheds
🔧 The small repairs finished
🤝 Facts to share, never a target value
📉 A plan in case the value comes in low
