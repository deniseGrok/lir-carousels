## Slide copy
1. [Band: LISTING SOON? PREP FIRST.] FIX THE SMALL STUFF BEFORE BUYERS SEE IT. "Most of what buyers notice can be handled before the sign goes up. Here's the prep order I recommend."
2. THE MARKET, SouthPark, as of September 2026: median list $625,000; median sold $610,000; 573 active homes (+17.90% YoY); 53 median days on market. "More listings than a year ago means buyers have more to compare." Source: Realtor.com Economic Research, SouthPark page, Sep 2026 (A)
3. WALK IT LIKE A BUYER: start at the curb, then the front door, then every room; write down scuffs, drips, sticky doors and dead bulbs; ask a friend to do the same walk (my tips)
4. CLEAR, CLEAN, THEN PAINT: pack what you won't need before moving day; deep-clean kitchens, baths and windows; minor cosmetic updates like paint, fixtures and landscaping typically pay off (A)
5. FIND PROBLEMS BEFORE THE BUYER DOES: consider a pre-listing inspection; fix anything leaking, broken or unsafe; skip the big remodel, since major renovations rarely return full cost (A)
6. NC DISCLOSURES: START THEM NOW: NC owners give buyers the Residential Property and Owners' Association Disclosure Statement (B); plus the Mineral and Oil and Gas Rights Disclosure (C); gather HOA dues, repair records and warranties while you prep (my tip)
7. SC SELLERS AND OLDER HOMES: SC owners deliver a written disclosure statement before the contract is signed (D); built before 1978, federal rules require a lead-based paint disclosure, EPA's pamphlet, and a 10-day inspection window buyers can waive (E)
8. GET IT PHOTO-READY: open the blinds and turn on every light; clear counters, tables and the fridge door; tidy the yard and front entry (my tips)
9. PRICE FROM THE SOLDS: start with recent nearby sales of similar homes, not list prices; factor in condition after your prep; pricing accuracy directly affects buyer interest and days on market (A)
10. MY RECOMMENDATION: START PREP 30 DAYS OUT. "Walk it, fix it and start your disclosures. Then let's price it from the solds together." + contact block: Denise Wilson | REALTOR® / Lifestyle International Realty / denisewilson.lircarolinas.com / 704-615-7347 / Equal Housing Opportunity
