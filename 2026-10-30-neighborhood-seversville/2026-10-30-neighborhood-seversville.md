## Sources (fetched/verified Oct 30, 2026, ~4:45–5:00 AM ET)
- Realtor.com Economic Research RDC_Inventory_Core_Metrics_Zip.csv (econdata.s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/Reports/Core/, Last-Modified Sep 30 2026 14:41 GMT; latest month 202609), ZIP 28208, month 202609: median listing price $385,723; median list price/sq ft $270; active listings 210; median days on market 74; quality_flag 0. ZIP 28208 covers a large part of west Charlotte beyond Seversville, so the slide labels the numbers by ZIP, not as Seversville-only.
- historysouth.org/jcsu/ (Tom Hanchett, Levine Museum of the New South; updated Jan 2025): a rural settlement known as Seversville clustered along the south side of West Trade Street; Henry G. Severs (1842–1915) ran a country store; Bruns Avenue, Duckworth Avenue, Auten Street and Katonah Avenue are in the old Seversville vicinity.
- hl.mecknc.gov Savona Mill (Designated Historic Landmarks, Charlotte / West End; 528 South Turner Ave., 28208): Savona Manufacturing Co. incorporated 1908, fine finished textiles using cotton damask weaving; established by 1914, expanded to a South Turner Ave. property two miles northwest of downtown adjacent to the Piedmont and Northern Railway line; one-story Weave Mill completed 1916, designed by Lockwood Greene; 1921 three-story Spinning Mill (Richard C. Biberstein) doubled the operation; textile production ceased 1934; Old Dominion Box Co. acquired it 1935; 1951 Paper Warehouse addition; operated until the 1990s.
- NC DNCR press release Apr 29 2015 "National Register Adds 18 North Carolina Places": Savona Mill listed 12/02/14; 1921–1922 spinning mill doubled the size; 1951 addition with distinctive mushroom-shaped columns. (Also NC SHPO nomination MK2211, NRIS 14000989.)
- portmanholdings.com, Oct 16 2025 (BSA Design lease) + Apr 14 2025 (first office tenant): Savona Mill fronts the Stewart Creek Greenway and offers 180,000 sq ft of creative office space and 20,000 sq ft of ground-floor retail.
- Wikipedia "Bruns Avenue station" + "CityLynx Gold Line": Bruns Avenue stop on West Trade St. between S. and N. Bruns Ave., serving Seversville; opened Aug 30 2021 (Phase 2); shelters house art installations by George Bates.
- charlottenc.gov CATS Art in Transit, Rail Art: George Bates, "The Worth of That, is That Which It Contains and That is This, and This With Thee Remains", 2021, West End windscreen art glass blending present-day depictions with research from the JCSU archives, yearbooks and the Charlotte Library.
- parkandrec.mecknc.gov Greenways: Irwin Creek, Stewart Creek and Wesley Heights Greenways, 2.7 miles from State Street to Cedar Street with a connection to Freedom Drive.
- Axios Charlotte Mar 28 2025 (Ashley Mahoney) + engage.mecknc.gov Stewart Creek Greenway ("officially opened March 2025"): 181-foot bridge, the longest in the county's greenway system, next to Savona Mill; ~200 ft boardwalk each side near State St.; skyline views heading toward Uptown.
- Tip slide: general guidance (FEMA flood map, elevation certificate, City of Charlotte rezoning petitions); no claim that any specific home is in a floodplain.
- Cut: Blue Blaze Brewing (Axios Mar 2025 says it "used to be" there, i.e. closed); spindle/loom/worker counts (hl.mecknc.gov gives 30,000 spindles installed in 1921 but 18,000 spindles by 1925, inconsistent); Vera at Savona Mill apartments + Phase 2 (third-party multifamily, plans unverified on a primary source); Huber Engineered Woods lease (paywalled CBJ); Lakewood trolley revival idea (2019, speculative); Fort Apache fire station; all racial/demographic history of Seversville and Western Heights (Fair Housing: no demographic language); church history (religion); Bruns Academy / schools; crime; population.
