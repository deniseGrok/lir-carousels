## Slides
1. MURALS, MUSIC AND A MAIN STREET. PLAZA MIDWOOD. Just outside Uptown, east of Hawthorne Lane; founded in 1910 as a streetcar suburb; murals on nearly every corner (A, B)
2. A HISTORIC DISTRICT. Streets laid out by the Oakland Land Company; Local Historic District since 1992; covers The Plaza, Thomas Ave and parts of Pecan and Clement; overseen by the Historic District Commission (B)
3. LIVE MUSIC AFTER DARK. Snug Harbor and Petra's (jazz, indie, rock and more); local talent and touring artists; open mic nights and drag shows at Legion Brewing (A)
4. EAT YOUR WAY DOWN CENTRAL. Villani's Bakery (Italian); milkbread walk-up window (donuts to crispy chicken); Calle Sol (ceviche, empanadas); Midwood Smokehouse; Supperland (A)
5. A NEIGHBORHOOD BEER CRAWL. Pilot Brewing, Resident Brewing Company, Burial Beer Co., Southern Strain Brewing Company (A)
6. WALK IT, RIDE IT. Walkable tree-lined streets; Joy Rides e-bike rentals; Veterans Park green space (A)
7. SHOP LOCAL. House of Africa; Betty by Moxie Mercantile; Pinhouse (duckpin bowling, self-pour taps); Boris & Natasha; Lunchbox Records (A)
8. GETTING AROUND. Main roads Central Ave, Hawthorne Lane, The Plaza (B); CityLYNX Gold Line streetcar ends at Sunnyside Ave on Hawthorne Lane; 17 stops, 4 miles, through Center City to the Historic West End (C, D)
9. WHAT HOMES COST HERE (Realtor.com, as of Sep 2026): median list $820,000; median sold $912,500; $434/sq ft; 45 active listings; median 35 days on market (E)
10. START ON CENTRAL AVE.
