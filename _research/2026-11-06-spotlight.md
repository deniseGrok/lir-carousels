### Slide 1 — COVER
- **Place:** Belmont, North Carolina (Gaston County).
- **Hook line options:** “Railroad. Mills. River.” or “Garibaldi. Mills. Gardens.”
- **Intro (draft):** A historic mill town on the Catawba peninsula, about ten miles west of Charlotte.
  - Sources: Belmont Historic District NR nomination (NPS Form 10-900), narrative — “eastern Gaston County… peninsula formed by the Catawba River… approximately ten miles west of Charlotte” (`_research/GS0024.pdf` / `GS0024-full.txt`; nomination dated for Keeper entry Dec. 27, 1996 per NPGallery). City of Belmont About page confirms peninsula setting (Catawba east, Lake Wylie south, South Fork west) — https://www.cityofbelmont.org/about/ (fetched Nov 6, 2026).

### Slide 2 — THE TOWN / LOCATION
Pill: `THE TOWN` | H1 idea: `A PENINSULA|[o]WEST OF CHARLOTTE.[/o]`
- Belmont sits in eastern Gaston County on a peninsula formed by the Catawba River and the South Fork of the Catawba River. — NR nomination Section 8 / description (`GS0024.pdf`); City About page (same geography; also names Lake Wylie to the south).
- Roughly ten miles west of Charlotte; Gastonia (county seat) about five miles west. — NR nomination description (`GS0024.pdf`).
- ZIP for market slide: **28012**. — Realtor.com Economic Research ZIP file (below); City park pages use Belmont, NC 28012.

### Slide 3 — THE HISTORY
Pill: `THE HISTORY` | H1 idea: `FROM GARIBALDI|[o]TO BELMONT.[/o]`
- In 1870, engineer John Garibaldi oversaw a fueling station and railroad bridge site on the South Fork peninsula; the stop became known as Garibaldi or Garibaldi’s Station. — Belmont Historic District NR nomination, Statement of Significance (`GS0024.pdf`, fetched Nov 6, 2026).
- By 1873, the Atlanta and Charlotte Airline was completed between Charlotte and Spartanburg through that stop (period of significance for the historic district begins 1873). — Same NR nomination. **Do not use “1871”** (City About says 1871; conflicts with NR — CUT city’s 1871 wording).
- The community was renamed Belmont in 1886. — NR nomination (Abbot Leo Haid petitioned a rename; St. Mary’s rejected; Belmont adopted 1886). City About also states rename in 1886.
- Belmont was incorporated in 1895. — City About https://www.cityofbelmont.org/about/; NR nomination (“Incorporated in 1895”); NPGallery significant years include 1895 (NRIS 96001525).
- Chronicle Mills, organized/chartered 1901, was the first textile mill in Belmont (NCSU: chartered March 18, 1901; first cotton opened Feb. 28, 1902). — City About; NCSU Textiles History https://history.textiles.ncsu.edu/textile-companies/the-chronicle-mills/ (fetched Nov 6, 2026); NR nomination (“organization of Chronicle Mills in 1901”).

### Slide 4 — HISTORIC DOWNTOWN / DISTRICT
Pill: `THE DISTRICT` | H1 idea: `264 BUILDINGS|[o]ON THE REGISTER.[/o]`
- Belmont Historic District listed in the National Register of Historic Places on **December 27, 1996** (NRIS **96001525**). — NPGallery https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/96001525 (Date Published 12/27/1996); City of Belmont Historic District page https://www.cityofbelmont.org/belmont-historic-district/ (fetched Nov 6, 2026) says listed in 1996 with **264 contributing buildings**.
- Nomination resource count: 264 contributing buildings (+ 59 noncontributing buildings; 1 contributing site). — `GS0024.pdf` cover sheet.
- District roughly bounded by (former) Sacred Heart College campus, railroad line, N. and S. Main, Glenway, Bryant Sts., Keener Blvd., Central Ave. — NPGallery location string; City map page.
- Architecture styles noted by NPS: Colonial Revival, Tudor Revival, Bungalow/Craftsman. — NPGallery NRIS 96001525.
- Downtown commercial core concentrates along Main Street between Catawba Street and the Southern Railway corridor; early-20th-century brick storefronts with corbelled cornices, parapets, recessed display windows. — NR nomination narrative description (`GS0024.pdf`).
- **NR listing alone does not restrict private owners** unless they pursue rehabilitation tax credits. — City Historic District page (fetched Nov 6, 2026). Useful for tip slide too.

### Slide 5 — DANIEL STOWE CONSERVANCY / GARDENS
Pill: `THE GARDENS` | H1 idea: `380 ACRES|[o]ON LAKE WYLIE.[/o]`
- Daniel Stowe Conservancy: **380 acres** of conserved land open to the public, Belmont address **6500 South New Hope Road, Belmont, NC 28012**, about 20 miles from Uptown Charlotte on the shores of Lake Wylie. — https://danielstoweconservancy.org/about-us/ (fetched Nov 6, 2026); saved `_research/stowe-about.html`.
- Botanical gardens set aside 1991 and **opened in 1999** by Daniel J. Stowe; Gardens are the centerpiece (~90 acres of the 380). — Same About page.
- **8+ miles of trails** on the property; trails connect to the Carolina Thread Trail system. — About page (“over eight miles”; Spring 2025 crushed-concrete additions). Thread Trail: Stowe Connector Trail is a **1.2-mile** crushed-stone/natural trail from the Conservancy primary parking area; with Duke Kimbrell Trail forms a Conservancy perimeter loop; only those two trails are in the Thread Trail network. — https://www.carolinathreadtrailmap.org/trails/trail/stowe-connector-trail (fetched Nov 6, 2026); hours dawn–dusk; no fee.
- Trailhead Store + scenic trails: **open daily 7 a.m.–7 p.m.**; trails, Trailhead Store, Farmhouse Garden Center, and dog park are **free**; **only The Gardens at Stowe require admission or membership**. — https://danielstoweconservancy.org/visit/hoursandadmission/ (fetched Nov 6, 2026); saved `_research/stowe-hours.html`.
- Gardens admission (general day pass, in person): Adult **$18.95 + tax**; Senior 60+ **$16.95 + tax**; Child 2–12 **$10.95 + tax**; under 2 free; members free (event fees may apply). — Same hours/admission page. **Fees can change — re-check before build.**
- CUT: specific Garden open-day schedule (page loads hours dynamically; not captured as static text Nov 6). CUT: Prairie Castle Playground “opening soon” status.

### Slide 8 — MY TIP
Pill: `MY TIP` | H1 idea: `CHECK CITY LIMITS|[o]ON THE TAX BILL.[/o]`
- Property taxes in Belmont are managed, billed, and collected by **Gaston County**. Bills go out in July and are due by January 1. — City FAQ https://www.cityofbelmont.org/question/how-do-i-pay-my-property-taxes/ (fetched Nov 6, 2026); saved `_research/city-taxes-faq.html`.
- “All property tax bills include both county and city taxes, if you live within a municipality’s corporate limits.” — Same City FAQ.
- Confirm whether a parcel is inside Belmont corporate limits (City Limits Map document on city site) and review the Gaston County Tax Office listing before budgeting carrying costs. — https://www.cityofbelmont.org/documents/city-limits-map/; Gaston Tax Office link from the FAQ: https://www.gastongov.com/government/departments/tax_office.php.
- Optional second bullet (riverfront buyers): Inside Belmont city limits, floodplain reviews for Special Flood Hazard Area work are handled by **Gaston County Planning and Development Services** (effective July 1, 2019). — https://www.cityofbelmont.org/question/who-handles-flood-plain-reviews-in-the-city-of-belmont/ (fetched Nov 6, 2026). Pair with FEMA/NC flood map check — do not invent zone letters for specific streets.
- Alt tip (also verified): National Register listing alone does not protect or restrict private property unless the owner seeks rehab tax credits (15% state credit path for owner-occupied; federal/state path for income-producing). — City Historic District page.
