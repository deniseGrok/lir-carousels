Hours

The Gardens at Stowe + Big Leaf Café

Please note that final admission to The Gardens is a half hour before close each day and that general admission day passes must be purchased in-person at Stowe.

Adventure Trail + Prairie Castle Playground

Visitors can enjoy the Adventure Trail + Prairie Castle Playground during open Garden hours.

The Butterfly Bungalow

The Butterfly Bungalow is located in The Gardens and open 10 a.m.-3 p.m. Wednesday-Sunday from Memorial Day to Labor Day.

Please note that the Bungalow is closed on Wednesdays from 11:30 a.m.-12 p.m. for the Butterfly Release Experience. 

The Trailhead Store + Trails 

Open daily 7 a.m. – 7 p.m.

The Farmhouse Garden Center 

9 a.m.-5 p.m. Wednesday-Sunday

Closed Monday + Tuesday

Admission to The Gardens

Please note that The Trailhead Store, The Farmhouse Garden Center, Dog Park and our scenic trails are free to the public! Only The Gardens at Stowe require admission or membership. Admission must be purchased in-person at Stowe. 

To learn more about reciprocal or discounted admission, click here. 

Adult: $18.95 +tax
Senior: (60+) : $16.95 +tax
Child: (2-12) : $10.95 +tax
Children Under 2: Free

Please note, special event admission fees may apply to select events.

Garden Members: Free

(Fees may apply to specific events + workshops)
