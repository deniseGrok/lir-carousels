## Slide copy (10)
1. Cover — Discover Concord / Harmony. Cotton. Racing. / The Cabarrus County seat on the I-85 corridor, founded in 1796.
2. THE ORIGINS — A TOWN NAMED FOR HARMONY. — founded 1796 on 26-acre site as seat of new Cabarrus County, incorporated 1806; "harmony" name after compromise over county-seat site, main street = Union Street; courthouse fire 1875, new courthouse 1876 = Historic Cabarrus County Courthouse.
3. THE MILLS — COTTON, RAIL AND MILLS. — NC Railroad just west of town → cotton buying/shipping hub after Civil War; J. M. Odell bought old Concord Cotton Factory 1877, three more mills by 1892; J. W. Cannon first textile company 1887, new plant near railroad 1892.
4. THE DISTRICT — 190 PROPERTIES ON NORTH UNION. — NRHP Apr 15 1986 (NRIS 86000789); 190 properties, 25-block area, ~1 mile along tree-lined N. Union St. toward downtown; Greek Revival, Italianate, Queen Anne, Colonial Revival, bungalow.
5. THE PARK — 238 ACRES AND A 10-ACRE LAKE. — Frank Liske Park 238 acres (Cabarrus County), former working farm, refurbished dairy barn + silo; 10-acre lake fishing/paddle boats, walking/fitness trails, nature trail, 18-hole mini golf; Hector H. Henry II Greenway along Rocky River, three segments, boardwalks + observation decks.
6. THE SPEEDWAY — RACING HERE SINCE 1960. — Charlotte Motor Speedway on Concord Pkwy S, 1.5-mile superspeedway, first World 600 June 19 1960; 2,000-plus acres, Dirt Track at Charlotte 2000, zMAX Dragway 2008; 2.28-mi ROVAL debuted 2018.
7. THE SEASON — HOLIDAYS ON UNION STREET. — 29th Tree Lighting Fri Nov 20 6–9 p.m. Downtown Concord, free; 98th Christmas Parade Sat Nov 21 2:30 p.m., ends Union St + Corban Ave; Speedway Christmas Nov 20 2026–Jan 3 2027 (ticketed).
8. MY TIP — CHECK FOR A LOCAL DISTRICT. — North + South Union Street = National Register districts, Edgewood = locally designated; local districts: many exterior changes need a Certificate of Appropriateness from the Historic Preservation Commission; confirm district status + read design standards (siding, windows, porches, roofs, fences, trees) before offering.
9. THE MARKET — REALTOR.COM • ZIP 28025 • OCT. 2026 — $339,900 median list · $200/sq ft · 214 active · 58 median DOM. Source line: "Source: Realtor.com Economic Research, ZIP 28025 (Concord NC, incl. downtown) monthly listing data, October 2026; viewed Nov. 8, 2026".
10. MY RECOMMENDATION — LET'S TOUR IT TOGETHER. — walk North Union Street and downtown, check district rules, compare homes; contact card Denise Wilson | Broker/REALTOR®, Lifestyle International Realty — Carolinas, denisewilson.lircarolinas.com, 704-615-7347, EHO.
