## Instagram
🔐 Wiring money for your closing? Pause before you hit send.

Scammers send fake wiring instructions that look like they came from your agent or closing attorney.

📧 Would you spot a look-alike email address?
📞 Do you have a trusted phone number for your closing attorney's office?
🚩 Would a last-minute change to the wire details make you stop?
🏦 Do you know where your funds should go, and in whose name?
✅ Will you call to confirm your money arrived?

A few minutes on the phone before and after you wire can help protect the money you worked hard to save.

Email can be faked. A call to a number you already trust is much harder to fake.

## Facebook personal profile
🔐 Friends, if you're buying a home and getting close to closing day, please read this one before you wire any money!

Here's what I walk my buyers through before they send their closing funds:

📧 Scammers can send fake wiring instructions that look like they came from your agent or closing attorney
📞 Before you wire, call the closing attorney's office at a number you already trust, never one from the email
🚩 Treat any change to the wire instructions as a red flag until you verify it by phone
🏦 Keep account numbers and money details off email
✅ After you wire, call again to confirm your funds arrived, and if something feels wrong, call your bank right away

A quick phone call before and after you wire can help protect the money you worked so hard to save. 💛
