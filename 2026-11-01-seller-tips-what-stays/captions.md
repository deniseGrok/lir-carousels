## Instagram
🏡 Getting ready to sell… do you know exactly what stays with the house?

Before you list, it helps to decide what goes with the sale and what goes with you.

💡 Which light fixtures and ceiling fans stay?
🌳 What about the shed, the plants, and the EV charger?
📱 Are your smart devices ready to unpair and reset?
📄 Is anything leased or not fully paid off?
🛋️ Which freestanding items, if any, are part of the deal?

Clear answers up front help prevent surprises at the final walk-through and at closing.

Moving out is busy. A clear list keeps it simple.
