🏡 Inherited a home and thinking about selling it?

Before you list, it helps to know who can sign and what needs to be in place first.

✍️ Who has the authority to sign, the executor or the heirs?
📄 Do you have the paperwork that proves your right to the home?
🏦 Have you told the mortgage servicer in writing?
🛡️ Does the insurer know the home may sit empty?

## Facebook personal profile
🏡 Friends, if you've inherited a home and you're thinking about selling, this one's for you.

I know this often comes at a hard time. Here's what I walk sellers through before an inherited home goes on the market.

✍️ Confirm who has authority to sign: the executor, the heirs, or both
📄 Gather the will, death certificate, executor letters, and deed
🏦 Tell the mortgage servicer in writing and ask what proof it needs
🛡️ Let the insurer know about the death and any vacancy
💰 Learn your tax basis and talk with a CPA before you sell

These steps protect the estate, the property, and every owner's share.
