1. [Band: UNDER CONTRACT? PLAN THE CLOSE.] FROM CONTRACT TO KEYS. "The weeks between an accepted offer and closing day shape how smoothly you move in. Here's how I recommend using them." (framing)
2. THE MARKET, Charlotte, as of October 2026 (stats only, no photo): median list $421,450; sale-to-list 99% (1.16% under ask on average); 63 median days on market; 6,000 active (+13.79% YoY). "More homes to choose from than a year ago, and offers still land close to asking. Plan your closing as carefully as your offer." Source: Realtor.com Economic Research, Charlotte, NC, Oct 2026 (A)
3. CHECK YOUR RATE LOCK: a lock keeps your rate from changing until closing, if you close in time and your application doesn't change; locks typically run 30, 45 or 60 days, make sure yours covers your closing date; extending a lock can be expensive, so ask about it up front (B)
4. KEEP YOUR FINANCES STEADY: even a locked rate can change if your loan amount, credit score or verified income changes; a new loan or a missed payment can move your credit score (B); I recommend checking with your lender before any big purchase or job change (my recommendation)
5. GET A COPY OF THE APPRAISAL: on a first-lien mortgage, your lender must give you a free copy of every appraisal; it's due promptly when it's done, or three business days before closing, whichever is earlier; you may be asked to waive that timing, think it over before you agree (C, C2)
6. LINE UP INSURANCE EARLY: lenders generally require proof of homeowner's insurance; standard policies don't cover flood or earthquake damage, though you may be able to add it; you can shop for your own policy and compare it with your Loan Estimate (D; flood also E)
7. READ YOUR CLOSING DISCLOSURE: your lender must give it to you at least three business days before closing; compare it with your Loan Estimate: loan type, rate, monthly payment and fees; by law, only certain costs can change, ask about anything that did (F, G)
8. VERIFY EVERY WIRE: an email with last-minute changes to wire instructions may be a scam; confirm by phone with trusted contacts, using numbers you saved in advance; never use phone numbers or links from the email itself (G, H)
9. GET READY FOR CLOSING DAY: bring your ID, your Closing Disclosure and your payment or proof of wire; take all the time you need to read every document (G); NC: keys, codes and garage openers are delivered at Closing unless an agreement says otherwise (Form 2-T) (I)
10. MY RECOMMENDATION: BUILD ONE CLOSING CALENDAR. "I recommend putting your rate lock, appraisal, insurance and Closing Disclosure dates on one calendar the day you go under contract.
