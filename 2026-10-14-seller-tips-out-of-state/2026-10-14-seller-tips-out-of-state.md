## Slides
1. [Band: MOVED AWAY? STILL OWN HERE?] You can sell a SouthPark home without living here
2. SouthPark Sep 2026: median list $625,000; median sold $610,000; 573 active; 53 median days on market (B)
3. Start the disclosure first: G.S. 47E requires the RPOADS before any offer; buyer may cancel otherwise (C, D)
4. Answer every question Y/N/NR/NA; answers are the owner's; brokers can't complete it (C)
5. Keep it current: update the Disclosure when you learn something new (C) + my tip on vacant-home checks
6. Line up the closing attorney: licensed NC attorney supervises residential closings (E)
7. Nonresident: buyer files NC-1099NRS within 15 days of closing, gives seller a copy (F)
8. NC taxes: nonresident seller files NC return, tax on the gain (G); see a tax pro
9. Be there without being there: my remote process (opinion, labeled)
10. My recommendation: start 60 days out + contact box
