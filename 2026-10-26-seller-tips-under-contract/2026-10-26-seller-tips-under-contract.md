## Facts and sources (fetched and read Oct 26 2026, ~4:25-4:35 AM ET)
A. N.C. G.S. 47E-7 (Change in circumstances): after delivering a disclosure statement, if the owner discovers a material inaccuracy, or a statement is rendered inaccurate in a material way by an event or circumstance, the owner shall promptly correct it by delivering a corrected statement; the statute references sale agreements that require the property to be in substantially the same condition at closing as on the offer date, reasonable wear and tear excepted. https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_47E/GS_47E-7.html (slides 5, 6)
B. S.C. Code 27-50-50(A): the owner delivers the disclosure form before a real estate contract is signed by purchaser and owner, or as otherwise agreed in the contract. https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t27c050.php (slide 7)
C. S.C. Code 27-50-60: if the owner discovers a material inaccuracy after delivery, or an event makes it materially inaccurate, the owner shall promptly correct it by delivering a corrected statement or make reasonable repairs before closing. https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t27c050.php (slide 7)
