## Slide map
1. [Band: BEFORE YOU TOUR. GET PRE-APPROVED.] Know your number before you fall for a house. Preapproval letter = what a lender is tentatively willing to lend (A)
2. THE MARKET: SouthPark, as of September 2026: median list $625,000; median sold $610,000; 573 active; 53 median days on market (B)
3. Prequal vs. preapproval: both are letters based on assumptions; some lenders prequalify on what you report and preapprove on verified info; neither is a guaranteed loan offer (C)
4. Build your lender packet: pay stubs (last 30 days); W-2s + signed federal returns (2 yrs); 2 most recent bank statements; down payment source + photo ID; needs vary by lender (D)
5. What a strong letter tells a seller: sellers frequently require one before accepting an offer (A); should state lender reviewed credit, income and cash to close (E); ask what assumptions were made (A)
6. Two NC deposits to plan for: earnest money (common, not required, held in trust by an escrow agent) (E); due diligence fee (negotiated, paid directly to the seller, generally non-refundable, credited at closing) (E, F)
7. Closing costs + your attorney: appraisal, title insurance, government taxes, prepaids (G); NC licensed attorney supervises residential closings (H); SC closings supervised by an SC lawyer (I) and lender must ask borrower's attorney preference (J)
8. Tour with a written plan: price ceiling from your own budget; tell your agent your top price (A); must-haves list (my tip, labeled)
9. Don't fall first: touring before you know your number; letting max approval set the budget; letter expiry typically 30 to 60 days (A) + my tip (labeled)
10. My recommendation: Letter first, then the tour + contact box (Denise Wilson | REALTOR® · Lifestyle International Realty — Carolinas · 704-615-7347 · denisewilson.lircarolinas.com · Photos are illustrative. Equal Housing Opportunity.)
