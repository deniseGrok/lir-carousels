## LinkedIn
A seller's sale price and a seller's net are two different numbers, and I recommend knowing both before setting a list price. Here's why. The payoff on a mortgage is the amount to pay the loan in full on a specific date, not the balance on the last statement, and the servicer must send it within seven business days of a written request, with limited exceptions. In North Carolina, the excise tax is $1 per $500 of the price, paid by the seller. In South Carolina, the deed recording fee is $1.85 per $500, and it's the seller's responsibility. Under NC Form 2-T, property taxes are prorated on a calendar-year basis, and sellers pay certain HOA resale and transfer fees. Commissions aren't set by law; they're negotiated and put in writing. And the IRS lets many owners exclude up to $250,000 of gain on a main home, or $500,000 filing jointly.
