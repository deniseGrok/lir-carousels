## Slide copy
1. [Band: SELLING? KNOW YOUR NET.] YOUR PRICE ISN'T YOUR NET. "What you walk away with is the price minus your loan payoff, closing costs and anything you agree to give. Here's how I recommend estimating it before you list." (framing)
2. THE MARKET, Charlotte metro, September 2026, NC + SC (stats only, no photo): median list $424,950 (-3.1% YoY); active 11,182 (+10.3% YoY); median days on market 64; price cuts 26.2% of listings. "More competition than a year ago, and about 1 in 4 listings had a price cut. Knowing your net helps you price with confidence." Source: Realtor.com Economic Research, Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia, NC-SC metro, Sep 2026 (A)
3. REQUEST YOUR PAYOFF EARLY: payoff isn't the statement balance, it's what it takes to pay the loan in full on a specific date; servicer must send it within 7 business days of a written request, with limited exceptions (B); under NC Form 2-T, loans and liens, like a home equity line, are paid at or before closing (C ¶8(f))
4. PLAN FOR TRANSFER TAX: NC excise tax $1 per $500, paid by the seller, $800 on a $400,000 sale (D); SC deed recording fee $1.85 per $500, the seller's responsibility, $1,480 on a $400,000 sale (E); both paid at closing from proceeds (C ¶8(i) for NC)
5. EXPECT PRORATIONS: under NC Form 2-T, property taxes prorated on a calendar-year basis; seller responsible for taxes and HOA dues through the settlement date (C ¶10); already paid this year's tax bill? ask how it shows up on your settlement statement (my recommendation)
6. ASK ABOUT HOA FEES: ask early for the HOA's resale and transfer fees (my recommendation); under NC Form 2-T, sellers pay for resale certificates, payment-status letters and ownership transfer fees; buyers pay for lender questionnaires and move-in or working-capital charges (C ¶9)
7. COMMISSIONS ARE NEGOTIABLE: broker commissions aren't set by law, they're negotiated and put in writing; a buyer may ask you to help with their agent's pay as a concession, that's your decision (F); whatever you agree to comes out of your net, so see it on paper first (my recommendation)
8. COUNT REPAIRS AND CREDITS: repairs you agree to after inspections are part of your cost to sell; so is closing-cost help or a home warranty you agree to pay for; under NC Form 2-T, a seller-paid home warranty is paid at Settlement (C ¶8(m), 8(n))
9. KNOW THE TAX RULES: exclude up to $250,000 of gain on a main home, or $500,000 filing jointly; generally owned and lived there 2 of the last 5 years; get a Form 1099-S? report the sale even if the gain is excluded; a tax professional can help (G)
10. MY RECOMMENDATION: GET A NET SHEET FIRST. "I recommend a written estimate of your net before we set your list price, because it shows the number that matters before you decide.
