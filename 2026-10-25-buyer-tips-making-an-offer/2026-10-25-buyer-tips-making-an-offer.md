## Facts and sources (fetched and read Oct 25 2026, ~4:00-5:00 AM ET)
A. NCREC "Questions and Answers on: Offer and Acceptance": offer/accept/reject/counter; seller must sign without changes and you must be notified; any change is a counteroffer; you can withdraw until notified; until signed and notified, the seller can accept a competing offer even if yours came first; seller's broker may not divulge competing price/terms without the offering party's authority; don't assume special consideration; earnest money not required but common (good faith); brokers must deposit earnest money checks no later than three banking days after acceptance; NC sales contracts must be in writing. https://www.ncrec.gov/Brochures/OfferandAcceptancePrint.pdf (also 21 NCAC 58A .0116(b)(3))
B. NCREC "Questions and Answers on: Earnest Money Deposits": refund if the buyer terminates before the due diligence period ends; afterward generally forfeited unless the seller breaches/can't perform. https://www.ncrec.gov/Brochures/EarnestMoneyBrochure.pdf
C. NCREC "Questions and Answers on: Due Diligence": DD fee paid directly to the seller on the standard Offer to Purchase and Contract (Form 2-T); buys a negotiated period during which the buyer may terminate for any reason; non-refundable except for seller breach; typically credited to the buyer at closing; no DD fee is required by law. https://www.ncrec.gov/Brochures/DueDiligencePrint.pdf
D. S.C. Code 32-3-10 (Statute of Frauds): a contract for the sale of lands must be in writing and signed. https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t32c003.php
E. S.C. Code 40-57-135(I)(1),(5): licensees prepare all offers in writing and promptly present them; offer rejected without counter → commission rejection form signed by the licensee to the offeror within 48 hours. https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t40c057.php
F. S.C. Code 40-57-136(D)(1)(a)(ii): checks deposited in the trust account within 48 hours after written acceptance, excluding Saturday, Sunday and bank holidays. https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t40c057.php
