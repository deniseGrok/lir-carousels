## Facts and sources (verified Oct 30 2026)
A. NC home inspectors licensed by the NC Home Inspector Licensure Board (license required to inspect for compensation): G.S. Chapter 143, Article 9F https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/ByArticle/Chapter_143/Article_9F.html ; SC home inspectors licensed by the SC Residential Builders Commission (SC LLR) https://www.llr.sc.gov/res/homeinspector.aspx (slide 3)
B. Buyers inspect: SC S.C. Code 27-50-80 (article does not limit purchaser's obligation to inspect) https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t27c050.php ; NC REC 4.22 instructions (buyers strongly encouraged to obtain their own inspections; disclosure statement not a substitute) http://www.ncrec.gov/Forms/Consumer/rec422.pdf (slides 4, 9)
C. Disclosure forms cover roof, chimneys, floors, foundation, structural components; plumbing, electrical, heating, cooling and other mechanical systems: G.S. 47E-4(b)(2),(3) https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/ByChapter/Chapter_47E.html ; S.C. Code 27-50-40(A)(2),(3) (slide 5)
D. Owner's actual knowledge: G.S. 47E-4(a)(1) and (b); S.C. Code 27-50-40(B). NC brokers must disclose material facts they know or reasonably should know regardless of the owner's responses: REC 4.22 instructions; NCREC Bulletin "Rolling out the Revised RPOADS!" https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/rolling-out-the-revised-rpoads/ (slide 7)
E. NC owner may discharge the duty to disclose by attaching a written report by a home inspector or other expert; owner not liable for errors made in reasonable reliance (not grossly negligent): G.S. 47E-6 (slide 8)
